# Nyumbani Support Solutions — Website

Compassion. Dignity. Support. Home.

Static HTML/CSS/JS website for Nyumbani Support Solutions, an in-home care company serving families across Kenya.

## Seven Services
- Elderly Care at Home
- Post-Discharge Recovery
- Skilled Nursing & Oversight
- Dementia & Frailty Support
- Child & Family Autism Support
- Palliative & Comfort Care
- Equipment Rental Bundles

## Structure
- `index.html` — Homepage
- `pages/` — About, Services, Care Packages, Equipment, Family Resources, Contact, Privacy, Terms, individual service pages (including service-autism.html)
- `assets/` — Logo, favicons, caregiver and equipment photography
- `css/style.css` — Single stylesheet, Uzazi-aligned forest green + warm bronze palette
- `js/app.js` — Theme toggle, scroll reveals, FAQ accordions
