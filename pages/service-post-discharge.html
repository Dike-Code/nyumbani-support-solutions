Post-Discharge Recovery

From hospital to home — safely, with a plan

The days immediately after discharge are the highest-risk period of any recovery. Our structured post-discharge service bridges the gap between hospital and home — so your loved one heals faster, with fewer complications.

Request a Care Assessment View Care Packages
What's Included

Everything you need for a safe recovery at home

Our post-discharge service follows the client from the day they leave hospital through to stable, independent recovery.

Discharge Plan Follow-Through

We review hospital discharge notes and implement every instruction — ensuring nothing falls through the cracks between the ward and home.

Medication Reconciliation

Careful review of all medications — prescribed, over-the-counter, and herbal — to prevent dangerous interactions and ensure correct dosing from day one.

Vitals Monitoring

Regular blood pressure, pulse, temperature, and oxygen saturation checks — recorded and communicated to the family and treating physician.

Wound Monitoring

Post-surgical wound observation for signs of infection, poor healing, or complications — with escalation to a nurse or physician when needed.

Transportation to Follow-Ups

Coordination and escort for post-discharge clinic appointments — so no follow-up visit is missed and the care team stays informed.

Recovery Nutrition & Escalation

Meal support tailored to post-discharge dietary requirements — plus rapid escalation protocols if the client's condition deteriorates.

Is This Right for You?

When post-discharge care is essential

  • Your loved one is coming home after surgery — hip replacement, abdominal, cardiac, or orthopedic
  • A family member has had a stroke and needs close monitoring and rehabilitation coordination
  • A senior is leaving hospital after a fracture and cannot safely manage alone
  • The discharge came earlier than expected and the family is not ready to manage all care alone
  • Complex medications have been introduced and the family needs help with reconciliation
  • The hospital team recommended professional home-care follow-up before the next appointment
How We Deliver This Care

A clear recovery pathway

1

Pre-Discharge Planning

We engage with the hospital team before discharge — reviewing notes, medication lists, and any specific instructions.

2

Day One at Home

A nurse or trained caregiver arrives on discharge day — establishing a vitals baseline, confirming medications, and setting up the home for safe recovery.

3

Structured Recovery Phase

Scheduled daily or bi-daily visits with wound checks, vitals recording, medication management, and family updates throughout.

4

Step-Down & Handover

As recovery stabilises, we right-size care — transitioning to less frequent visits and empowering the family to manage confidently.

Common Equipment We Bring

Ready from day one

Depending on the discharge plan, we can coordinate and deliver the right equipment to the home before your loved one arrives.

Hospital Bed

Adjustable height and backrest for safe transfers and comfortable recovery positioning.

Commode & Toilet Rail

Reduces fall risk during nighttime bathroom visits in the early days of recovery.

Wheelchair or Walker

For clients with limited mobility — chosen based on the level of post-operative restriction.

Pressure-Relief Mattress

For clients on bed rest — reduces pressure ulcer risk during extended recovery periods.
