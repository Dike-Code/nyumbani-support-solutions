The right equipment for safe recovery at home

Every item in our catalog is hospital-grade, regularly maintained, and delivered clean. We don't just drop it at your door — we set it up correctly, show the family how to use it safely, and make sure it fits the recovery situation properly.

Care-Scenario Bundles

Equipment matched to your recovery situation

The right bundle means nothing missing and nothing unnecessary. We'll confirm which is right after the assessment.

Post-Surgery Recovery

Everything needed after major orthopaedic, abdominal, or cardiac surgery at home.

  • Adjustable hospital bed
  • Pressure-relief mattress
  • Commode & toilet safety rail
  • Walker or crutches
  • Over-bed table

Respiratory Support

For clients managing COPD, pneumonia, or chronic respiratory conditions at home.

  • Oxygen concentrator
  • Portable oxygen cylinder
  • Pulse oximeter
  • Nebuliser
  • Positioning wedge pillow

Mobility & Independence

For clients recovering from stroke, hip fracture, or orthopaedic procedures.

  • Standard or power wheelchair
  • Walker / rollator
  • Quad cane
  • Grab rails (bathroom, corridor)
  • Non-slip bath mat & shower chair

Comfort & Palliative

For clients receiving palliative or long-term comfort care at home.

  • Pressure-relief air mattress
  • Comfort recliner chair
  • Positioning wedges & bolsters
  • Bedside table & call bell
  • Patient lift (where needed)
Individual Equipment

Available items — rental or bundle

All items include delivery, professional setup, and family training on correct use.

Adjustable Hospital Bed

Electric or manual height-adjustable bed with backrest and foot-elevation controls. Includes safety rails, pressure-relief base, and correct positioning instruction.

Includes setup + family training

Electric Wheelchair

Powered wheelchair with joystick control, properly fitted to the client. Includes battery handover, charging instruction, and family training on safe transfer technique and footrest adjustment.

Includes setup + family training

Walker / Rollator

Standard walker or wheeled rollator adjusted to correct height for the client. Ideal for clients with limited balance who are working towards independent walking.

Includes setup + family training

Commode Chair

Bedside commode with arms and splash guard for clients who cannot safely reach the bathroom. Foldable options available for smaller spaces.

Includes setup + family training

Oxygen Concentrator

Stationary oxygen concentrator providing continuous supplemental oxygen for clients with respiratory conditions. Family trained on flow settings, alarms, and safety protocols.

Includes setup + family training

Portable Oxygen Cylinder

Lightweight portable cylinder for use during outings and appointments. Includes regulator, tubing, and instruction on safe storage and refill coordination.

Includes setup + family training

Patient Lift

Manual or electric hoist for safe transfer of non-weight-bearing clients between bed, chair, or commode. Family trained on sling attachment, weight limits, and emergency procedures.

Includes setup + family training

Pressure-Relief Mattress

Alternating-air or foam pressure-relief mattress that sits on the hospital or home bed. Prevents pressure ulcers for clients on extended bed rest or with reduced mobility.

Includes setup + family training

Comfort Recliner Chair

High-back recliner with armrests and footrest — designed for palliative clients who spend significant time sitting but need positional variety to reduce discomfort.

Includes setup + family training
How Equipment Delivery Works

From assessment to setup in your home

1

Needs Assessment

We review the discharge plan, medical notes, and home layout to confirm which equipment is needed, where it should go, and whether there are any access or power requirements to plan for.

2

Bundle Confirmed & Scheduled

We confirm the rental bundle, timeline, and any additional items — and schedule delivery to arrive before or on the day of discharge, so everything is ready when your loved one comes home.

3

Delivery & Setup

Our team delivers, assembles, and positions every item — adjusting bed height, installing safety rails, checking oxygen concentrator function, and positioning mobility aids correctly.

4

Family Training

We walk the family through how to use every piece of equipment — including safe transfer technique, oxygen equipment safety, alarm responses, and who to call if anything changes.

Equipment enquiries welcome — no commitment needed

Not sure what equipment your loved one will need? Contact us with the discharge diagnosis and we'll recommend the right items, confirm availability, and give you a clear cost before anything is confirmed.
