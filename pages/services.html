Our Care

Seven focused services, built for Kenyan families

Whether your loved one is coming home from hospital, aging in place, or navigating a complex diagnosis — we have a structured care service shaped for that exact situation. Every service is delivered at home, by credentialed caregivers who show up consistently.

Elderly Care at Home

Daily companionship, bathing, dressing, mobility, meals, and medication reminders — delivered with patience and dignity for seniors who want to age in place.

Post-Discharge Recovery

Structured support after surgery, stroke, fractures, or hospital admission — nurse oversight, medication reconciliation, and a safer road to recovery.

Skilled Nursing & Oversight

Dementia & Frailty Support

Gentle, dignity-centered support for seniors living with memory decline, frailty, fall risk, or reduced independence — with family education built in.

How It Works

Starting care is simple

Four clear steps from your first enquiry to ongoing, professional care at home.

1

Reach Out

Send us a message, email, or request a callback. Tell us briefly what kind of support your loved one needs — there are no wrong answers at this stage.

2

Free Care Assessment

We visit at home — or speak by phone — to understand the medical situation, the household layout, the family's schedule, and any equipment needs. No cost, no obligation.

3

Custom Care Plan

We recommend the right services, equipment, and a care package shaped around your specific needs, timeline, and budget. You decide what to move forward with — at your pace.

4

Care Begins — At Home

Your trained caregiver arrives in uniform. We handle coordination, communicate with the family regularly, and adapt the plan as your loved one's situation evolves.
